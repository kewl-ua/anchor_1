# Art credits

## Ground textures (`assets/terrain`)

Made from CC0 photo textures (public domain: free for any use, commercial
included, no attribution required; credited here all the same). Each one was
cropped, made seamless, shrunk to 256×256, recoloured to its terrain's colour
and cut down to a small palette by `tools/pixelize` (see
`tools/make_terrain.sh` for the exact settings).
